What this site is for

Imagine handing your partner a link instead of a stack of documents. They open it on their phone or laptop and see, in plain language:

  • what vaire* digital sells and how you price it,
  • how projects run from first hello to launch day,
  • the numbers you’re aiming for in Year 1, and
  • a clear month‑by‑month path from “junior dev” to “running a real agency”.

The full site will hold all of that detail. This landing page is simply the cover of the book — a welcoming front door that sets expectations before you scroll into the deeper sections.

How it feels to use

Every page will read like a guided walkthrough, not software documentation. Big headings mark the major parts of the plan — foundation, services, operations, roadmap, risks, growth — and under each one you’ll see short paragraphs and checklists you can skim at 11 pm without getting lost.

The font controls at the top are there because this is meant to be read, not glanced at. Bump the text up to “A+” and the layout gently adapts so it still feels like a real page, not a zoomed‑in spreadsheet.

On this site, you’ll always know:

  • what you owe each other in the partnership,
  • what you promise every client in plain words,
  • what “good” looks like for your first 12 months, and
  • where to find any checklist, script, or template you need.

What we’ll add after you pick a design

Once you choose the layout you like best, this landing page will grow into the front of a full internal site:

  • an overview section that summarises the whole plan in one sitting,
  • gentle, book‑like chapter pages for services, operations, roadmap, and risks,
  • checklists you can actually tick off as you work through launches, and
  • a dedicated “tools & prompts” area for your day‑to‑day dev work in Cursor.

For now, treat this page as the “Chapter 0” of your blueprint: a calm place to arrive, breathe, and remember why you’re building vaire* digital in the first place.
