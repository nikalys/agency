Reading view

A distraction‑free way to live with your business plan.

This layout is built for long reading sessions: one column, large type, generous line‑height, and nothing on the page that doesn’t help you understand what vaire* digital is trying to become.

What this site promises you

When the full plan is wired into this layout, every section — services, operations, roadmap, risks — will feel like a chapter of the same book. You won’t see tables squeezed into sidebars or bullets pretending to be paragraphs. You’ll see sentences, examples, and concrete next steps you can act on.

The goal is simple: you and your partner should be able to open any page, read for five minutes, and close the laptop knowing what to do next week.

Why the font controls matter here

The A‑ / A / A+ controls at the top make a much bigger difference in this view. In “A+”, text moves toward book‑size reading, while spacing, margins, and line‑length quietly adjust so paragraphs still feel balanced rather than oversized.

This is the layout to pick if you care most about comfort on laptops and tablets, and you want the plan to feel like a calm Sunday‑afternoon read instead of a dashboard.
