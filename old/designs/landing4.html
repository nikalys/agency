Internal plan · Reading copy

The story of vaire* digital, told like a book — not a spec sheet.

You’re not building a giant agency. You’re building a small, steady web studio that fits around your life, your learning curve, and a 50/50 partnership. This site is where that story lives in long‑form, with space to breathe between ideas.

Why turn the plan into a website?

PDFs are great for printing; terrible for actually coming back to every week. By turning the full business plan into a website you get:

  • type that scales up for late‑night reading,
  • clear section breaks that feel like chapters, and
  • a single link you and your partner can share on any device.

What this first page needs to do

This landing page doesn’t need to teach every detail. It just needs to make you both think: “Yes, this feels like our business. Yes, I can follow this.” From here, the rest of the site will unfold into:

  • a foundation chapter (values, offers, pricing),
  • an operations chapter (how projects actually run),
  • a roadmap chapter (what to do each month), and
  • a tools chapter (checklists, prompts, and templates).
