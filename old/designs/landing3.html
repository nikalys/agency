Think of this site as a timeline you can walk along together — from defining what vaire* digital is, to selling your first projects, to hitting your Year‑1 income targets.

Foundation & services

You’ll start by agreeing on what kind of work you’re doing, who you’re doing it for, and how you’ll charge. The full plan turns this into a chapter that feels like a calm conversation, not a table of prices.

2

Operations & client experience

Next, you’ll walk through what happens from “new lead” to “site launched”, including checklists, call scripts, and email templates that live on their own pages you can open during real projects.

3

Development roadmap

The roadmap chapter is your learning plan: which skills to focus on in Weeks 1–2, how to ship your agency site in Weeks 3–6, and how to move into full‑price client work without feeling like you skipped steps.

Risks, numbers & growth paths

Finally, you’ll see the honest side of the plan: what can go wrong, what the money should look like month by month, and how you might grow in Year 2 once the basics feel boring.
