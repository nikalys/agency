Internal launch blueprint

One shared page that explains the whole business.

vaire* digital is a tiny GTA agency built on a junior developer + sales partner model. This website is the “source of truth” for that partnership — the place you come back to when you’re asking:

  • What exactly are we promising clients?
  • How much are we charging and why?
  • What does the next month of work look like?

The full site will break the answers into calm, readable chapters. This landing page just introduces the story and gives your partner confidence that “yes, this is a real, thought‑through plan”.

At a glance

Format
Portable website version of a 3‑part business plan (~100+ pages).
For
Junior dev in the GTA + partner who handles sales and marketing.
Focus
Small local businesses: trades, restaurants, clinics, real estate, services.
Goal
Reach healthy Year‑1 income while you learn, build systems, and avoid burnout.

What this page will grow into

After you choose a design, we’ll attach navigation and chapter pages: services & pricing, operations, roadmap, risks, and checklists — all in the same friendly tone you see here.

How you and your partner will actually use this

You’ll meet here before big decisions: new offers, price changes, marketing pushes, or just a “where are we at?” check‑in. Because the content is written in full sentences instead of tables and bullet dumps, it’s easy to read aloud and talk through — like a book you’re working from together, not a spec sheet.
