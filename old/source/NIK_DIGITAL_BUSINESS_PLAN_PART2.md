## 5. OPERATIONAL WORKFLOWS

### 5.1 Client Onboarding Process (Step-by-Step)

**PHASE 1: LEAD → QUALIFIED PROSPECT (Partner Handles)**

```
1. Lead comes in (ad, referral, website form, cold outreach)
2. Partner qualifies lead:
   □ Budget: Can afford $500+ minimum?
   □ Timeline: Realistic expectations (2-4 weeks)?
   □ Fit: Target market (local GTA business)?
   □ Authority: Decision maker or influencer?
3. If qualified: Partner books discovery call
4. If not qualified: Partner politely declines or refers elsewhere
```

**PHASE 2: DISCOVERY CALL (30 min - Partner + You)**

```
Attendees: Marketing partner (leads), you (technical questions), prospect
Goal: Understand needs, show expertise, set expectations

AGENDA:
├── Introductions (2 min)
├── Understand business goals (5 min) - Partner asks
├── Current pain points (5 min) - Partner explores
├── Review service needs (8 min) - Partner explains options
├── Technical questions (5 min) - You answer
├── Pricing discussion (3 min) - Partner presents
└── Next steps (2 min) - Partner closes or sets follow-up

POST-CALL:
- Partner sends follow-up email with:
  □ Proposed package or custom quote
  □ Timeline estimate
  □ Portfolio examples (similar projects)
  □ Clear CTA: Accept proposal or request changes
```

**PHASE 3: PROPOSAL & CLOSING (Partner Handles)**

```
1. Partner sends formal proposal (use template from Part 1)
2. Client reviews (give 3-5 days)
3. If questions: Partner answers (loops you in for technical)
4. If price negotiation: Partner consults you on feasibility
5. Once agreed:
   □ Partner sends contract (via email or DocuSign)
   □ Client signs electronically
   □ 50% deposit invoice sent immediately
```

**PHASE 4: PROJECT KICKOFF (You Take Over)**

```
TRIGGER: Deposit payment received

YOUR ACTIONS (within 24 hours):
□ Partner notifies you + shares project details
□ You send client welcome email (template below)
□ You send intake questionnaire (Google Form)
□ You schedule kickoff call (30 min, within 3 days)
□ You create project folder (Google Drive)
□ You add project to Trello/Notion
□ You set calendar reminders for milestones
```

## 6. SALES & CLIENT ACQUISITION STRATEGY

### 6.1 Lead Generation Channels (Priority Order)

**1. REFERRALS (Highest ROI, Warmest Leads)**
- Past clients (ask every project: "Who else needs a website?")
- Friends & family networks
- Local business networks (BNI, Chamber of Commerce)
- Partner's existing network

**Tactic:** Create referral incentive: 10% off next project for both referrer and referred client.

---

**2. LOCAL SEO (Long-term, Scalable)**
- Optimize YOUR agency website for "[city] web design" keywords
- Google Business Profile for your agency (Hamilton + surrounding cities)
- Local directories: Yelp, Yellow Pages, local business listings
- Google Maps visibility

**Tactic:** Write blog posts targeting "[city] + [service]" keywords, e.g., "Website Design for Hamilton Contractors"

---

**3. CONTENT MARKETING (Authority Building)**
- Blog posts on your agency site (SEO-focused)
- Case studies showcasing results (portfolio)
- How-to guides for small businesses ("Do You Need a Website? 5 Signs")
- Guest posts on local business blogs

**Tactic:** Publish 2-4 blog posts per month during first 6 months to build SEO momentum.

---

**4. SOCIAL MEDIA (Awareness & Social Proof)**
- **LinkedIn:** B2B networking, showcase work, connect with local business owners
- **Instagram:** Visual portfolio, before/after comparisons, behind-the-scenes
- **Facebook Groups:** Join local business communities, provide value (not spam)
- **YouTube:** Tutorial videos (optional, time-intensive)

**Tactic:** Post 2-3x per week on LinkedIn + Instagram. Share client wins, tips, behind-the-scenes.

---

**5. PAID ADS (Optional, Budget-Dependent)**
- Google Ads: Target "website design [city]" searches
- Facebook/Instagram Ads: Target local business owners (demographics + interests)
- Retargeting: Show ads to people who visited your site but didn't convert

**Tactic:** Start with $200-300/month budget once you have 5+ portfolio pieces. Partner handles ad spend.

---

**6. COLD OUTREACH (Last Resort, Partner-Led)**
- Email outreach (personalized, value-first, not spammy)
- LinkedIn connection requests + DMs (warm intro style)
- Phone calls (only with warm introduction or clear opt-in)

**Tactic:** Partner handles this. You focus on delivery. Only do cold outreach if other channels aren't producing.

### 6.4 Handling Objections (Quick Reference)

**"Too expensive"**
→ "I understand. Most agencies charge $3,000-5,000 for this. We're at $1,200 because we use efficient tools. If budget is tight, we could start with a 3-page landing page for $500, then add more later. Would that work?"

**"I can use Wix for $20/month"**
→ "You could! The difference is time and expertise. DIY takes 20-30 hours to learn. Plus we handle SEO, speed optimization, and lead generation—not just pretty design. What's your time worth?"

**"I want to think about it"**
→ "Totally fair. Can I ask what specifically you're unsure about? ... I'll send you [case study/testimonial] so you can see results we've delivered. When's a good time to follow up? How about [specific day]?"

**"Need to talk to my partner"**
