## 🎯 HOW TO USE THIS PACKAGE

### **PHASE 1: Planning (Week 1)**
1. Read all 3 parts cover-to-cover (yes, all of it)
2. Take notes on anything unclear
3. Print the checklists (or save to Google Docs)
4. Discuss with your marketing partner
   - Confirm 50/50 split
   - Align on pricing
   - Clarify roles
5. Make any adjustments to fit your specific situation

### **PHASE 2: Setup (Week 2)**
1. Follow "Business Launch Checklist" (Part 3, Section 12)
2. Set up all tools and accounts
3. Register business (or plan to after first clients)
4. Create sales collateral from templates provided

### **PHASE 3: Portfolio Building (Weeks 3-6)**
1. Follow "Development Roadmap" (Part 3, Section 8)
2. Build 3 portfolio pieces (real or fictional)
3. Launch your agency website
4. Partner starts warm outreach

### **PHASE 4: First Clients (Weeks 7-12)**
1. Use "Operational Workflows" (Part 2) for every project
2. Follow email templates and checklists
3. Track your time meticulously
4. Request testimonials from every client
5. Refine processes based on learnings

### **PHASE 5: Growth (Month 4+)**
1. Follow "Month-by-Month Action Plan" (Part 3, Section 13)
2. Track "Success Metrics" (Part 3, Section 14) monthly
3. Refer back to guide when you hit obstacles
4. Scale using strategies in Part 3, Section 10
