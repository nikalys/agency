Services, Pricing & What Clients Get

Your complete menu of offers — landing pages, small sites, SEO, maintenance, and three tiered packages. Instead of a grid of numbers, this reads like a story about what each offer actually feels like for a client and why your pricing makes sense.

Pricing Philosophy

Your pricing is built on five core principles. First, value-based pricing — clients pay for results and business value, not your hours. Second, AI efficiency pass-through — tools make you faster, so clients benefit from lower prices. Third, transparent bundling — package savings are shown clearly to encourage bundling. Fourth, flexibility within limits — you can adjust based on client budget, but you have minimums. Fifth, no hidden fees — all costs are disclosed upfront.

Your advantage is clear: using AI tools like Claude Code and Cursor, you can deliver in 15 hours what traditionally takes 30–40 hours. This lets you charge below market rate while maintaining healthy margins. Clients get professional results at 40–60% of agency prices, and you still hit effective hourly rates of $30–40+ as you get faster.

Payment Terms & Policies

Standard Payment Structure: 50% deposit required before work begins (non-refundable, covers setup time), 50% balance due upon project completion and client approval, Net 15 payment terms (balance due within 15 days of final invoice).

Alternative for Larger Projects ($2,000+): 33% deposit to start work, 33% at midpoint (design approval stage), 34% at completion.

Monthly Retainers: Billed monthly in advance on the 1st of each month, auto-pay preferred (credit card on file via Stripe), 30-day cancellation notice required, pro-rated first month if starting mid-month.

Late Payment Policy: Days 1–15 are payment period (no penalties). Days 16–30 get a reminder email sent (no penalties yet). Day 31+ gets 5% late fee applied, and website access may be suspended. Day 60+ initiates collection process, small claims court if necessary.

Refund Policy: Deposits are non-refundable (covers initial research, setup, and opportunity cost). Full refunds only if project not started within 14 days of deposit (your fault). Partial refunds for work completed but not used (calculated pro-rata). No refunds for completed, approved work that client later changes mind about. Dispute resolution: good faith negotiation first, mediation if needed.

What Happens if Client Doesn't Pay Final Balance: Week 1 gets a friendly reminder email. Week 2 gets formal payment request email plus phone call. Week 3 gets notice of intent to restrict site access. Week 4 gets website taken offline OR login screen placed on site. Week 6 gets collection agency referral or small claims court filing.

Scope Management: Unlimited minor revisions for 30 days (color changes, text edits, image swaps). Major changes (layout redesign, new pages, new features) equal new quote at $75/hour. After 30-day period, all changes billed at $75/hour OR switch to maintenance retainer.

Currency & Payment Methods: All prices in Canadian Dollars (CAD). Accepted payment methods include e-transfer (preferred, instant, no fees), Stripe (credit/debit cards, 3% fee passed to client), PayPal (available, 3.5% fee passed to client), cash (in-person meetings only, receipt provided), and cheque (business accounts only, 3–5 day hold period).
