What is Material Design? ▼

Material Design is a design system developed by Google that combines the classic principles of good design with innovation and technology. It's a visual language that synthesizes classic principles of good design with the innovation and possibility of technology and science.

Key Principles ▼

Material Design is based on three key principles: Bold, graphic, intentional. Typography, color, and images guide users through the product. Meaningful motion provides feedback and guides attention.

Elevation & Shadow ▼

Objects at different elevations cast shadows. The shadow conveys the degree of elevation between surfaces. Shadows are key to understanding spatial relationships in Material Design interfaces.
