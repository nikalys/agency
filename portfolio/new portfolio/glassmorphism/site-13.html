Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. We'll adjust billing accordingly for the remainder of your engagement.

What's included in revisions?

Revisions cover design concept changes and adjustments. Additional services like content changes or scope expansions may be billed separately.

How long does a project take?

Timelines vary by plan and project complexity. Starter projects typically take 3 months, Professional 6 weeks, and Enterprise timelines are custom.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes! We offer flexible payment schedules. Typically 50% upfront and 50% on delivery, but we can customize terms based on your needs.

What about ongoing support?

All plans include ongoing support for the term of the engagement. Enterprise clients get dedicated support beyond project delivery.

Is there a contract?

We use simple, fair contracts that protect both parties. Nothing hidden—just clear terms and mutual expectations laid out upfront.
